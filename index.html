Paloma T. de Lima

I am an associate professor at the Norwegian School of Economics (NHH) and at the IT University of Copenhagen (ITU).

My research interests revolve around structural graph theory, algorithms and parameterized complexity. I am also interested in problems arising in computational social choice and algorithmic game theory.

I was previously an assistant professor at ITU (2022 - 2024). Before that, I was a postdoc (2020 - 2021) and a PhD student (2016 - 2019) at the University of Bergen. I was fortunate to have Pinar Heggernes as my supervisor.
